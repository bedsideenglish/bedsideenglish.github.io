Model interview

Full transcript

  1. Doctor

    Mr. Baker, I'm Dr. Ellis. What's brought you in?

  2. Patient

    My big toe woke me up in the middle of the night. It's red, swollen, and incredibly painful.

  3. Doctor

    Tell me exactly how it started.

  4. Patient

    I went to bed feeling fine, then around three in the morning I woke up because my toe was throbbing. Even the bedsheet touching it was unbearable.

  5. Doctor

    Which joint exactly, and how does it look right now?

  6. Patient

    It's the big toe on my right foot. It's swollen, shiny, and bright red.

  7. Doctor

    On a scale of zero to ten, how bad is the pain?

  8. Patient

    It's a nine or ten. I can barely put weight on it.

  9. Doctor

    Have you eaten a lot of red meat or had much alcohol recently?

  10. Patient

    Actually, yes. I had a big steak dinner with several beers last night at a work event.

  11. Doctor

    Have you had a fever or felt generally unwell?

  12. Patient

    No, I don't think I have a fever, and other than the toe I feel okay.

  13. Doctor

    Has this happened before, in this or any other joint?

  14. Patient

    No, this is the first time I've ever had anything like this.

  15. Doctor

    Have you injured the toe recently, or had any cuts or wounds near it?

  16. Patient

    No, no injury and no cuts.

  17. Doctor

    Do you have any other medical conditions, like kidney problems or high blood pressure?

  18. Patient

    No, I'm generally healthy. No kidney problems that I know of.

  19. Doctor

    Do you take any regular medicines, including water pills or aspirin?

  20. Patient

    No, I don't take anything regularly.

  21. Doctor

    Any medicine allergies?

  22. Patient

    No, none that I know of.

  23. Doctor

    What do you think is causing this?

  24. Patient

    I think it might be gout, from the steak and beer. My uncle used to get something like this.

  25. Doctor

    What's worrying you most?

  26. Patient

    I'm worried this could be something serious, or that it might keep coming back or get worse.

  27. Doctor

    What were you hoping we could do today?

  28. Patient

    I want to know for certain what this is, and I want the right treatment started right away.

  29. Doctor

    Let me summarize. Your right big toe became suddenly red, swollen, and extremely painful overnight, following a meal of steak and alcohol, with no fever, injury, or previous episodes. Is that accurate, and is there anything else I should know?

  30. Patient

    That's exactly right. Nothing else.

  31. Doctor

    Thank you. This sudden pattern in the big toe after a rich meal and alcohol is very characteristic of gout, but because fever or infection can sometimes look similar, we'll examine the joint carefully and may take a small sample of fluid from it to confirm the crystals under the microscope and rule out infection at the same time. Once we've confirmed it, we'll start treatment to calm the inflammation quickly.

  32. Patient

    That sounds reasonable. I just want the pain to stop.

Flow at a glance

The route through the history

  1. Invite the patient's story
  2. Establish sudden nighttime onset
  3. Localize and characterize the joint
  4. Ask about dietary and alcohol triggers
  5. Screen for fever and prior episodes
  6. Rule out local injury or infection sources
  7. Review background, medicines, and allergies
  8. Summarize and explain the plan to confirm and treat

Do not miss

Questions that change the next step

  • Sudden, severe monoarticular pain waking the patient at night
  • Dietary purine and alcohol triggers
  • Fever or systemic illness that would suggest septic arthritis instead
  • Local skin break, wound, or spreading redness
  • Whether this is a first episode or a recurrence
