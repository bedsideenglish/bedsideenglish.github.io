Model interview

Full transcript

  1. Doctor

    Mr. Jin, I'm Dr. Parker. Keep the ankle supported while we talk. Walk me through exactly how you injured it.

  2. Patient

    About four hours ago I came down from a rebound in basketball. My right foot rolled inward, and I fell.

  3. Doctor

    Did you feel or hear a pop or crack when it happened?

  4. Patient

    No crack. I felt a sharp pull on the outside of the ankle.

  5. Doctor

    How quickly did the swelling and bruising appear?

  6. Patient

    It swelled within minutes. The bruising became obvious over the next couple of hours.

  7. Doctor

    Please point with one finger to where it hurts most.

  8. Patient

    Right over this outer ankle bone and just below it.

  9. Doctor

    Were you able to take four steps immediately afterward?

  10. Patient

    No. I managed two steps, then my teammates helped me off the court.

  11. Doctor

    Can you take four steps now, even with a limp?

  12. Patient

    No. Putting my full weight on it is too painful.

  13. Doctor

    Have you noticed any numbness or tingling in the foot?

  14. Patient

    No.

  15. Doctor

    Has the foot felt cold or looked unusually pale or blue?

  16. Patient

    No. The color and temperature seem normal.

  17. Doctor

    Was there any open wound or an obvious change in the shape of the ankle?

  18. Patient

    No open cut and no obvious deformity, just swelling.

  19. Doctor

    Have you injured this ankle before?

  20. Patient

    I had a mild sprain about three years ago, but it healed completely.

  21. Doctor

    What have you done for it since the injury?

  22. Patient

    I stopped playing, put ice on it, and kept it raised. I haven't taken pain medicine.

  23. Doctor

    How painful is it while supported, and what makes the pain worse?

  24. Patient

    It's about four out of ten while I keep it still, but it becomes sharp and much worse if I move the ankle or try to stand.

  25. Doctor

    Do you have any major medical condition or take any regular medicine?

  26. Patient

    No medical problems and no regular medicine.

  27. Doctor

    Do you have any medication allergies?

  28. Patient

    No known medication allergies.

  29. Doctor

    What activities are most important for you to get back to?

  30. Patient

    I sit at a computer for work, but basketball is what I'm worried about. I want to know whether it's broken and when I can play again.

  31. Doctor

    You rolled the right foot inward four hours ago and developed rapid swelling, bruising, and pain over the outer ankle. You could not take four steps then and still cannot now, but you report no wound, deformity, numbness, or change in foot color or temperature. Is that accurate, and is there anything else I missed?

  32. Patient

    That's all correct.

  33. Doctor

    I'll examine the bones, ligaments, movement, circulation, and sensation. Because you cannot take four steps and have pain near the ankle bone, an X-ray may be appropriate after the examination. We'll protect the ankle now and base return to basketball on the findings and your recovery, not on a fixed date.

  34. Patient

    Okay. I appreciate the clear answer.

Flow at a glance

The route through the history

  1. Reconstruct the landing and direction of force
  2. Establish immediate swelling and pain location
  3. Ask separately about four-step weight bearing
  4. Screen for open injury, deformity, and neurovascular change
  5. Review prior injury and self-care
  6. Identify work and sport goals
  7. Summarize before examination and imaging decision

Do not miss

Questions that change the next step

  • Exact mechanism and direction of the twist
  • Ability to take four steps immediately and during assessment
  • Point pain over the malleoli and other Ottawa-rule locations during examination
  • Open wound, deformity, severe pain out of proportion, numbness, or a cold or discolored foot
  • Prior injury and the patient's functional and return-to-sport goals
