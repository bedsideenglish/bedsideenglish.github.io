“Does the pain radiate anywhere?” is correct English and a correct clinical question. It is also a word many patients do not use about their own body.

They will answer you. That is the problem — you cannot tell whether they understood the question or just wanted to be helpful.

Three questions here, each in the clinical wording and in wording the patient already has. Neither version is wrong. One of them gets you an answer you can rely on.
