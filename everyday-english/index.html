Why these pages are different

A practice loop, not a phrase list

Each guide answers the search question immediately, then asks you to use the skill under listening pressure.

1 · Hear

Listen before the transcript is available. Replays and normal-speed playback remain useful, visible repair choices.

2 · Commit

Enter the exact details you believe you heard. The answer cannot drift after the transcript appears.

3 · Verify and transfer

See each detail scored, study one natural repair, then reuse it in a different everyday setting.
