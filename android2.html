Quick questions

Can I try it without an API key?

Yes. Demo mode lets you explore the guided practice flow without an API key or account.

How do full AI conversations work?

When you are ready for live conversations, the app guides you through connecting a provider. Your key stays on your device.

Is this a medical device?

No. Bedside English is an English-speaking practice tool, not medical advice or a clinical decision tool.
