01 · HOME

It opens with an instruction, not a feed.

No infinite scroll, no streak-bait. One daily mission, chosen from what your last sessions actually got wrong. Today it's four corrections that came due — so that's what the button says.

Adaptive daily mission · roadmap · streak
02 · PRACTICE

Eleven rooms. All of them uncomfortable.

A full scored patient encounter when you have twenty minutes. A two-minute empathy drill when you have two. Residency interview grilling, teach-back, listening lab, or a scenario you write yourself.

11 modes · every one of them scored
03 · PATIENT ENCOUNTER

The checklist ticks while you're still talking.

SOCRATES, red flags, ICE, safety-netting — tracked live, item by item, as you speak. You find out you skipped the closure during the encounter, when you can still go back for it. Not on a report afterwards.

Live rubric · SOAP note · unscripted AI patient
04 · SURVIVAL ENGLISH

The hallway. The pharmacy callback. 7 p.m. in the lab.

The moments that actually humiliate you abroad aren't in the OSCE. Flip on Surprise me and the scene stays hidden until someone starts talking. Flip on Ear-only and the transcript disappears — just a voice, an accent you pick, and you.

Surprise me · Ear-only · accent & pace control
05 · PRONUNCIATION LAB

It hears the /θ/ you've been skipping for ten years.

Nobody at work will correct your stethoscope. They'll just quietly decide you're hard to follow. Every session ends by depositing your slips here — with the sound you made, and the one you were reaching for.

Automatic · clinical terms included
06 · SRS REVIEW

Yesterday's mistake, back at your throat tomorrow.

Every slip becomes a card on a spaced-repetition schedule. And it isn't a flashcard you read — it's a transfer challenge: say the fix out loud, in a situation you've never seen. Export the whole deck to Anki whenever you want.

Spaced repetition · say-it-aloud · Anki export
07 · HISTORY & COST

Progress you can chart. A bill you can audit.

Every scored session, searchable, with the score trend over time. And because it runs on your own API key instead of a subscription, each session prints what it cost you — to the fourth decimal place, straight from the provider. Nothing rounded, nothing hidden, and no bill from me, because I never see any of it.

Score trend · your own token-level cost
08 / THE LOOP

Why it actually sticks.

Most language apps end at the score. This one treats the score as the input to tomorrow.

01

You speak

A live AI patient answers your words — not a branching script. Go off-book and it follows you.

02

It scores

Clinical checklist, grammar, rapport, intelligibility, speaking pace. Plus a SOAP note of what you took.

03

It keeps the slip

Every grammar error and mispronunciation is saved with the attending-level rewrite beside it.

04

It comes back

On a spaced schedule, in a new situation, out loud — until you stop making it. Then it stops asking.

09 / WHAT IT COSTS YOU
$0

I don't want your money.
Or your email.

No subscription. No account, no login, no ads, no telemetry, no analytics, no server of mine anywhere in the middle — so there is nothing to bill you for and nothing to collect about you. Your speech and transcripts go straight from your device to the AI provider you configure; I never receive them. The whole app is open source; read every line of it.

It works with Google's Gemini API using your own key, which Google gives out free. Gemini is the default and the only key you need to get started; the free tier covers ordinary daily practice. If you ever push past it, you pay Google directly at Google's price — and the app prints the exact cost of every session in the History tab so you're never guessing. Advanced users can also configure optional OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, or Microsoft Azure Speech features in Preferences; those providers handle requests under their own policies.

10 / THE REST OF IT

Things you'd only find out on day three.

Demo Mode needs no key

Three scripted patients, full feedback loop, zero setup and zero cost — so you can judge it before you sign up for anything.

Zero telemetry, by design

Sessions, mistakes and audio stay on the device. No account, no analytics, no crash pings, no cloud of mine. We never receive your data — AI requests go directly from your device to the provider you configure.

Onboarding in 8 languages

Get set up in your own language. Once you start, the practice itself stays English, always.

Exports to Anki

Your mistake deck belongs to you. Take it out whenever you want, in a format your existing routine already reads.

Nothing is ever lost

Drop the call, kill the app, lose signal mid-sentence — the transcript survives and you can analyze the session as-is.

Open source, all of it

Read the prompts, read the scoring, read exactly what gets sent where. Then build it yourself if you'd rather.
