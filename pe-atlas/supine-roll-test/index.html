Supine roll test: horizontal-canal BPPV

Observe positional horizontal nystagmus when horizontal-canal BPPV is suspected.

See the examination

Head supported on 30° raised backrest; eyes open

RIGHT turn: observe eyes ≥30 s (time compressed)

Return to centre; let symptoms settle

LEFT turn: observe direction, duration and symptoms

Return supported; compare the two observed patterns

How to do it

  1. Explain possible vertigo, obtain consent, and check safe cervical movement and ability to lie back. Use an appropriate modified test or specialist assessment if movement is unsafe.
  2. This raised-backrest variant starts supine on a couch raised about 30 degrees at the head, with the head supported and centred.
  3. Guide the head about 90 degrees to one side as tolerated and observe eye direction, duration and symptoms for at least 30 seconds, longer when indicated. Keep the head supported.
  4. Return to centre, let symptoms settle, then test the other side in the same way. Record the pattern on both sides, not just which turn felt worse.

What to say to the patient

  1. I will support your head and turn it to each side. Keep your eyes open and tell me if you feel spinning or pain.

A positive finding

Direction-changing horizontal positional nystagmus can support horizontal-canal BPPV. Geotropic and apogeotropic patterns require different side interpretation; symptom intensity alone does not identify the affected ear.

A negative finding

No observed nystagmus does not exclude intermittent BPPV; fixation and technique affect observation. Reassess atypical findings and other causes of vertigo.

Evidence & limitations

AAO-HNS recommends a supine roll test when the history fits BPPV but Dix–Hallpike produces horizontal or no nystagmus.

Points to remember

  1. Do not force a painful or restricted neck; this is an assessment, not an Epley treatment sequence.
  2. The supported turns and return are illustrated. Eye movements, canal particles, exact clinical speed and the full observation interval are not simulated.
