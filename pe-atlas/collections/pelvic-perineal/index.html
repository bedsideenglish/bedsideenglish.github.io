Pelvic, genital & rectal examination

7 techniques. Study separately indicated surface and internal examinations with consent, privacy and diagnostic limits.

  • Scrotal inspection · lie, asymmetry and skinInspect the scrotum and recognize concerning appearances without requiring a classic pattern before urgent assessment.Pelvic, genital & rectal examination↗
  • Testicular and epididymal palpationDistinguish testis, epididymis and cord while gently locating tenderness or a lump.Pelvic, genital & rectal examination↗
  • Saddle sensation · selected perineal light-touch sitesElicit subjective sensory change and, when indicated, compare selected perineal sites without using a normal result to exclude CES.Pelvic, genital & rectal examination↗
  • Anal sphincter tone · resting resistance and squeezeDistinguish resting resistance from voluntary squeeze when an internal examination is indicated; understand its major diagnostic limits in suspected CES.Pelvic, genital & rectal examination↗
  • Digital rectal examination · rectal walls and prostateTeach the actual reachable rectal and posterior prostate contacts separately from the existing positioning-only card.Pelvic, genital & rectal examination↗
  • Speculum examination · vaginal route and cervical viewDistinguish external landmarks, closed-blade insertion, controlled opening, the cervical view and safe withdrawal.Pelvic, genital & rectal examination↗
  • Bimanual pelvic examination · cervix, uterus and adnexaShow how vaginal fingers and an abdominal hand cooperate, without confusing cervical, uterine and adnexal assessment.Pelvic, genital & rectal examination↗
