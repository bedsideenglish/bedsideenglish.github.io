  • Orthostatic blood pressure and pulse기립성 혈압·맥박 측정Check the blood-pressure response to standing in a patient who can stand safely.안전하게 설 수 있는 환자에서 기립에 따른 혈압 변화를 확인합니다.General examination전신 진찰↗
  • Work of breathing and tripod posture호흡 노력·보조호흡근·삼각자세Observe respiratory effort and the posture the patient chooses before disturbing them.
