Skin examination

2 techniques. Describe lesions, pressure response and documented change.

  • Blanching response · petechiae and purpuraDescribe a rash and compare vascular redness that fades under transparent pressure with extravasated blood that remains visible. This is a physical sign, not a meningitis exclusion test.Skin examination↗
  • Pigmented lesion · ABCDE and documented changeDescribe a pigmented lesion systematically and identify concerning change. The drawings illustrate features, not proven melanoma, a dermoscopy image or a diagnostic score.Skin examination↗
