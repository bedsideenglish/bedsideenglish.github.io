Knee, ankle & calf

12 techniques. Explore knee stability and focused lower-leg examination.

  • Homans' signA traditional bedside screen for deep vein thrombosis (DVT) of the calf: calf pain on passive dorsiflexion of the foot. Know it because examiners and patients still mention it, but do not rely on it.Knee, ankle & calf↗
  • Anterior drawer test (knee)Tests the integrity of the anterior cruciate ligament (ACL). With the knee at 90 degrees and the foot fixed, an intact ACL stops the tibia from sliding forward under the femur. An abnormal forward glide with a soft endpoint suggests an ACL tear.Knee, ankle & calf↗
  • Lachman testChecks the integrity of the anterior cruciate ligament (ACL). With the knee in slight flexion, the ACL is the main restraint to forward translation of the tibia on the femur, so a torn ACL lets the tibia slide forward with a soft or absent end-point.Knee, ankle & calf↗
  • Thompson (Simmonds) squeeze testTests the continuity of the Achilles tendon. Squeezing a healthy calf pulls on the tendon and plantarflexes the foot; if the tendon is ruptured the pull is not transmitted and the foot does not move.Knee, ankle & calf↗
  • Ottawa ankle and foot rulesA decision rule that tells you whether an acutely injured ankle or midfoot needs an X-ray. You press four bony points and test weight-bearing; if none is positive, a fracture is very unlikely and imaging can be skipped.Knee, ankle & calf↗
  • Posterior drawer and posterior sag (PCL)Assess posterior tibial position and posterior cruciate ligament stability.Knee, ankle & calf↗
  • Valgus and varus stress of the kneeCompare collateral-ligament pain, laxity and endpoint at slight flexion and near extension.Knee, ankle & calf↗
  • Knee effusion: bulge sign and patellar tapLook and feel for intra-articular fluid, using two distinct techniques.Knee, ankle & calf↗
  • Anterior drawer of the ankle: ATFL assessmentAssess anterior talar laxity relative to the stabilised lower leg.Knee, ankle & calf↗
  • McMurray test — supported tibial rotationAssess meniscal symptoms with controlled knee flexion/extension and tibial rotation, using the external-rotation/valgus and internal-rotation/varus variant.Knee, ankle & calf↗
  • Apley compression/distraction testCompare knee symptoms during axial compression versus distraction with small tibial rotations in prone, recognising the limited specificity of that comparison.Knee, ankle & calf↗
  • Calf circumference · matched landmark levelMeasure both calves at the same landmark-defined level when evaluating unilateral swelling. The measurement contributes to clinical probability; it does not detect a thrombus.Knee, ankle & calf↗
