Prioritize the patient's condition. Do not require a routine physical examination or temperature check solely for vaccination in someone who appears healthy. Evaluate current symptoms or previous vaccine reactions when relevant. Acute shortness of breath or altered mental status takes priority and requires urgent assessment.

Physical examination checklists by clinical presentation

Examinations without a card remain in the checklist. An item that combines several examinations may include both available cards and specific techniques that are still pending. What you actually examine depends on the symptoms and clinical indications.

  1. 01

    Measure temperature to assess for fever

    Consider when symptoms or a separate assessment warrant it · not a required prevaccination test

    • Technique card pending
  2. 02

    General appearance (lethargy, dehydration, respiratory distress, or rash), pulse, and respiratory rate

    • General inspection from the end of the bed ↗
  3. 03

    Examine the throat and ears and auscultate the chest if symptoms warrant

    Select as clinically indicated

    • Oral and pharyngeal inspection and soft palate movement ↗
    • Posterior lung auscultation sequence ↗
    • Adult otoscopy: safe approach and tympanic membrane landmarks ↗
  4. 04

    Inspect the injection site: anterolateral thigh in infants and deltoid in older children and adults

    • Technique card pending
  5. 05

    For children, measure height and weight and assess overall development

    Consider when symptoms or a separate assessment warrant it · not a required prevaccination test

    • Technique card pending
