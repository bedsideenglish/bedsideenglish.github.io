Prioritize the patient's condition. Rapid cognitive decline, fluctuations in consciousness over the day, recent head trauma, a new severe headache, or one-sided neurologic deficits require prompt comprehensive assessment to evaluate for emergencies such as acute delirium, chronic subdural hematoma, or stroke.

Physical examination checklists by clinical presentation

Examinations without a card remain in the checklist. An item that combines several examinations may include both available cards and specific techniques that are still pending. What you actually examine depends on the symptoms and clinical indications.

  1. 01

    Assess orientation, memory, and attention with a brief cognitive screening test, such as the MMSE or MoCA

    • Technique card pending
  2. 02

    Vital signs and temperature

    • Radial pulse: rate and rhythm ↗
    • Upper-arm blood pressure: standard measurement and comparison of both arms ↗
    • Temperature measurement Technique card pending
  3. 03

    Neurologic examination for focal deficits or parkinsonian signs, such as rigidity and bradykinesia

    • Pronator drift test ↗
    • Patellar reflex (L2–L4, primarily L4) ↗
    • Plantar response: flexor and extensor (Babinski) ↗
    • Finger-to-nose test ↗
    • Assess bradykinesia and cogwheel rigidity Technique card pending
  4. 04

    Observe gait: stride length, foot dragging, and balance

    • Gait and tandem gait ↗
  5. 05

    Palpate the thyroid and assess skin findings and tendon reflexes

    • Patellar reflex (L2–L4, primarily L4) ↗
    • Thyroid palpation from behind while the patient swallows ↗
    • Look for skin findings associated with thyroid disease Technique card pending
