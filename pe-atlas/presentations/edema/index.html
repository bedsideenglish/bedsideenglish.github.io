Physical examination checklists by clinical presentation

Examinations without a card remain in the checklist. An item that combines several examinations may include both available cards and specific techniques that are still pending. What you actually examine depends on the symptoms and clinical indications.

  1. 01

    Measure blood pressure, pulse, oxygen saturation, and weight

    • Upper-arm blood pressure: standard measurement and comparison of both arms ↗
    • Pulse oximetry: placement, signal quality, and limitations ↗
    • Measure weight Technique card pending
  2. 02

    Press the shin or dorsum of the foot for at least 5 seconds to assess pitting, its extent, and symmetry

    • Assessment of pitting edema ↗
  3. 03

    Compare calf circumference and assess tenderness, erythema, and warmth

    • Calf circumference: measurement at the same reference level ↗
    • Assess focal calf tenderness, erythema, and warmth Technique card pending
  4. 04

    Assess neck vein distention, auscultate for S3, and check for basal lung crackles

    • Jugular venous pressure (JVP) assessment ↗
    • Cardiac auscultation areas (aortic, pulmonic, tricuspid, mitral) ↗
    • Posterior lung auscultation sequence ↗
    • Hepatojugular (abdominojugular) reflux ↗
  5. 05

    Assess abdominal distention, shifting dullness for ascites, hepatomegaly, jaundice, and spider angiomas

    • Shifting dullness ↗
    • Liver palpation with respiration ↗
    • Inspection of the lower palpebral conjunctiva and eyes for pallor or jaundice ↗
    • Inspect for spider angiomas and palmar erythema Technique card pending
  6. 06

    Inspect for periorbital edema and palpate the thyroid

    • Thyroid palpation from behind while the patient swallows ↗
    • Inspect for facial and periorbital edema Technique card pending
