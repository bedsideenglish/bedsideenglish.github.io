Prioritize the patient's condition. With signs of hypertensive emergency suggesting target-organ injury, such as severe headache, visual disturbance, tearing chest or back pain, unilateral weakness, or shortness of breath, prioritize immediate emergency care and assessment over completing the examination checklist.

Physical examination checklists by clinical presentation

Examinations without a card remain in the checklist. An item that combines several examinations may include both available cards and specific techniques that are still pending. What you actually examine depends on the symptoms and clinical indications.

  1. 01

    After 5 minutes of seated rest, measure blood pressure in both arms with an appropriately sized cuff; take at least two readings 1–2 minutes apart and average them, using the arm with the higher pressure

    • Upper-arm blood pressure: standard measurement and comparison of both arms ↗
  2. 02

    Height, weight, BMI, waist circumference, and pulse

    • Radial pulse: rate and rhythm ↗
    • Anthropometric measurements: height, weight, BMI, and waist circumference Technique card pending
  3. 03

    Perform fundoscopy for retinal vascular changes, hemorrhages or exudates, and optic disc edema

    • Direct ophthalmoscopy: approach and viewing the fundus ↗
  4. 04

    Assess apical impulse location, heart sounds including S4 or murmurs, lung crackles, neck vein distention, and leg edema

    • Precordial palpation: apical impulse, heaves, and thrills ↗
    • Cardiac auscultation areas (aortic, pulmonic, tricuspid, mitral) ↗
    • Posterior lung auscultation sequence ↗
    • Assessment of pitting edema ↗
    • Jugular venous pressure (JVP) assessment ↗
  5. 05

    Auscultate for cervical or abdominal bruits, and compare radial and femoral pulses simultaneously for delay

    • Radiofemoral delay ↗
    • Auscultate for abdominal and carotid bruits Technique card pending
  6. 06

    Palpate the thyroid and look for Cushing features, including a rounded face, wide purple striae, and bruising

    Select as clinically indicated

    • Thyroid palpation from behind while the patient swallows ↗
    • Inspect for Cushing features: a rounded face, purple striae, and bruising Technique card pending
