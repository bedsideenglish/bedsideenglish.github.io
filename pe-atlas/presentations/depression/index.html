Prioritize the patient's condition. If recent self-harm or immediate risk of self-harm is suspected, prioritize an individualized safety assessment and access to help. Altered consciousness, intoxication, or physical injury also requires physical assessment.

Physical examination checklists by clinical presentation

Examinations without a card remain in the checklist. An item that combines several examinations may include both available cards and specific techniques that are still pending. What you actually examine depends on the symptoms and clinical indications.

  1. 01

    Observe appearance, hygiene, facial expression, eye contact, and psychomotor activity

    • General inspection from the end of the bed ↗
  2. 02

    Rate and amount of speech, the patient's reported mood, and observed affect

    • Technique card pending
  3. 03

    Assess thought content, including suicidal thoughts or delusions of guilt, and perceptual disturbances

    • Technique card pending
  4. 04

    Assess orientation, memory, judgment, and insight

    • Technique card pending
  5. 05

    Assess symptom severity with a screening tool such as the PHQ-9

    • Technique card pending
  6. 06

    Check vital signs and palpate the thyroid

    Select as clinically indicated

    • Radial pulse: rate and rhythm ↗
    • Thyroid palpation from behind while the patient swallows ↗
    • Upper-arm blood pressure: standard measurement and comparison of both arms ↗
    • Temperature measurement Technique card pending
