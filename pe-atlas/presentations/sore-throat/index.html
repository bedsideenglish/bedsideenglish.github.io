Prioritize the patient's condition. Drooling, tripod positioning, trismus, or inspiratory stridor are critical signs of upper airway obstruction and raise concern for acute epiglottitis or deep neck infection. Do not stimulate the pharynx with a tongue depressor. Prepare immediately for airway management and emergency clinical assessment.

Examinations without a card remain in the checklist. An item that combines several examinations may include both available cards and specific techniques that are still pending. What you actually examine depends on the symptoms and clinical indications.

  2. 02

    Use a tongue depressor to inspect for tonsillar enlargement or exudate, uvular deviation, and soft palate swelling

    If epiglottitis is suspected, avoid tongue depressor examination and let the patient remain in a comfortable position
