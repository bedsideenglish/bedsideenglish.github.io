← Clinical presentations

Prioritize the patient's condition. Severe thirst with vomiting, abdominal pain, deep and rapid Kussmaul breathing, or decreased consciousness such as stupor suggests an acute metabolic emergency, including diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA). Immediate emergency assessment and a blood glucose check are needed.

Physical examination checklists by clinical presentation

Examinations without a card remain in the checklist. An item that combines several examinations may include both available cards and specific techniques that are still pending. What you actually examine depends on the symptoms and clinical indications.

  1. 01

    Check blood pressure, pulse, respiratory rate, and temperature for dehydration and Kussmaul breathing

    • Radial pulse: rate and rhythm ↗
    • Orthostatic blood pressure and pulse measurement ↗
    • Upper-arm blood pressure: standard measurement and comparison of both arms ↗
    • Respiratory rate, rhythm, and effort ↗
    • Temperature measurement Technique card pending
  2. 02

    Assess hydration using oral mucosal dryness and skin turgor

    • Oral and pharyngeal inspection and soft palate movement ↗
    • Orthostatic blood pressure and pulse measurement ↗
    • Capillary refill time at the fingertip pad ↗
    • Fluid status: oral mucosa and skin turgor ↗
  3. 03

    Measure height, weight, and waist circumference

  4. 04

    Check for acanthosis nigricans and skin infections

    Select as clinically indicated

    • Technique card pending
  5. 05

    Perform confrontation visual field testing if a pituitary lesion is suspected

    Select as clinically indicated
