Prioritize the patient's condition. For signs suggesting airway compression or involvement, such as breathlessness, inspiratory stridor, or rapidly developing dysphagia, stop the routine examination and proceed to immediate emergency airway assessment and care.

Physical examination checklists by clinical presentation

Examinations without a card remain in the checklist. An item that combines several examinations may include both available cards and specific techniques that are still pending. What you actually examine depends on the symptoms and clinical indications.

  1. 01

    Inspect the neck and assess mass movement while the patient swallows

    • Thyroid palpation from behind while the patient swallows ↗
    • Tongue protrusion test to assess for a thyroglossal duct cyst Technique card pending
  2. 02

    Palpate the mass for size, consistency, tenderness, mobility, and fixation

    • Cervical lymph node palpation ↗
  3. 03

    Palpate lymph nodes throughout the neck and in the supraclavicular, axillary, and inguinal regions

    • Cervical lymph node palpation ↗
    • Axillary lymph nodes: arm support and palpation by group ↗
    • Palpate inguinal lymph nodes Technique card pending
  4. 04

    Palpate the thyroid from behind

    • Thyroid palpation from behind while the patient swallows ↗
  5. 05

    Inspect the mouth, tonsils, and pharynx

    • Oral and pharyngeal inspection and soft palate movement ↗
