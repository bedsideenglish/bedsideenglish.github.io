Prioritize the patient's condition. An irreducible groin mass with severe tenderness, skin discoloration, peritoneal signs, or bowel obstruction symptoms has a high risk of strangulation. Never force reduction; arrange immediate urgent surgical assessment.

Physical examination checklists by clinical presentation

Examinations without a card remain in the checklist. An item that combines several examinations may include both available cards and specific techniques that are still pending. What you actually examine depends on the symptoms and clinical indications.

  1. 01

    Check temperature, pulse, and blood pressure for fever or tachycardia with incarceration or strangulation

    • General inspection from the end of the bed ↗
    • Radial pulse: rate and rhythm ↗
    • Upper-arm blood pressure: standard measurement and comparison of both arms ↗
    • Temperature measurement Technique card pending
  2. 02

    Inspect and compare both groins and the scrotum while standing, and assess bulging with coughing

    • Inguinal hernia examination: surface assessment ↗
  3. 03

    Palpate the mass for size, consistency, tenderness, and pulsation, and assess reducibility when supine

    • Inguinal hernia examination: surface assessment ↗
    • Lower limb peripheral pulses: femoral, popliteal, and pedal ↗
    • Further assessment of groin masses that are not hernias Technique card pending
  4. 04

    In male patients, invaginate the scrotal skin to place a finger at the inguinal canal opening and assess for an impulse with coughing

    • Inguinal hernia examination: surface assessment ↗
    • The original item's inguinal canal opening palpation through the scrotal skin Technique card pending
  5. 05

    Use the pubic tubercle and inguinal ligament as landmarks to assess whether a hernia is inguinal or femoral

    • Inguinal hernia examination: surface assessment ↗
  6. 06

    Inspect, auscultate, and palpate the abdomen for surgical scars, distention, bowel sounds, and tenderness

    • Abdominal inspection ↗
    • Abdominal auscultation: bowel sounds ↗
    • Light and deep abdominal palpation ↗
    • Rebound tenderness (Blumberg sign) ↗
  7. 07

    Scrotal examination and inguinal lymph node palpation

    • Scrotal inspection: position, asymmetry, and skin ↗
    • Testicular and epididymal palpation ↗
    • Palpate inguinal lymph nodes Technique card pending
    • Inguinal lymph node palpation Technique card pending
