Prioritize the patient's condition. With severe dehydration, inability to stand, signs of sepsis, or suspected peritonitis, stop the checklist examination and proceed to immediate emergency assessment of vital signs and clinical care.

Physical examination checklists by clinical presentation

Examinations without a card remain in the checklist. An item that combines several examinations may include both available cards and specific techniques that are still pending. What you actually examine depends on the symptoms and clinical indications.

  1. 01

    Check temperature, blood pressure, and pulse, including orthostatic changes

    • Radial pulse: rate and rhythm ↗
    • Orthostatic blood pressure and pulse measurement ↗
    • Upper-arm blood pressure: standard measurement and comparison of both arms ↗
    • Temperature measurement Technique card pending
  2. 02

    Assess hydration using oral mucosal dryness, skin turgor, and capillary refill time

    • Capillary refill time at the fingertip pad ↗
    • Fluid status: oral mucosa and skin turgor ↗
  3. 03

    Assess abdominal tenderness, bowel sounds, and distention

    • Abdominal inspection ↗
    • Abdominal auscultation: bowel sounds ↗
    • Light and deep abdominal palpation ↗
    • Rebound tenderness (Blumberg sign) ↗
  4. 04

    Perform an anal examination and digital rectal examination if indicated

    Select as clinically indicated

    • Digital rectal examination: consent, preparation, and left lateral positioning ↗
    • Digital rectal examination: rectal wall and prostate ↗
    • Detailed inspection of external anal lesions Technique card pending
