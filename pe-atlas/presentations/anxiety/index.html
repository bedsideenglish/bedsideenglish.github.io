Prioritize the patient's condition. With exertional chest pain, syncope, persistent arrhythmia, or severe breathlessness, do not assume anxiety; arrange immediate cardiovascular emergency assessment. If signs of self-harm risk are identified, secure support and address safety.

Physical examination checklists by clinical presentation

Examinations without a card remain in the checklist. An item that combines several examinations may include both available cards and specific techniques that are still pending. What you actually examine depends on the symptoms and clinical indications.

  1. 01

    Check pulse, blood pressure, respiratory rate, and temperature

    • Radial pulse: rate and rhythm ↗
    • Upper-arm blood pressure: standard measurement and comparison of both arms ↗
    • Respiratory rate, rhythm, and effort ↗
    • Temperature measurement Technique card pending
  2. 02

    Observe appearance, behavior, speech, and affect

    • General inspection from the end of the bed ↗
    • Mental status examination (MSE) and structured screening tools such as the GAD-7 Technique card pending
  3. 03

    Palpate the thyroid and assess for hand tremor and eye signs

    • Thyroid palpation from behind while the patient swallows ↗
    • Assess hand tremor and proptosis Technique card pending
  4. 04

    Auscultate the heart and lungs

    Select as clinically indicated

    • Cardiac auscultation areas (aortic, pulmonic, tricuspid, mitral) ↗
    • Posterior lung auscultation sequence ↗
  5. 05

    Assess symptom severity with a screening tool such as the GAD-7

    • Technique card pending
