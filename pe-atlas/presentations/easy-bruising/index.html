Prioritize the patient's condition. For signs suggesting major internal bleeding, such as severe headache, melena, hematemesis, or hematuria, or for hemodynamic instability, arrange urgent medical assessment and activate resuscitation support without delay.

Physical examination checklists by clinical presentation

Examinations without a card remain in the checklist. An item that combines several examinations may include both available cards and specific techniques that are still pending. What you actually examine depends on the symptoms and clinical indications.

  1. 01

    Assess vital signs: pulse and blood pressure for bleeding severity, and temperature for fever

    • Radial pulse: rate and rhythm ↗
    • Upper-arm blood pressure: standard measurement and comparison of both arms ↗
    • Temperature measurement Technique card pending
  2. 02

    Observe the distribution and size of bruises, petechiae, and purpura; assess for palpable purpura and bruises at different stages

    • Rash blanching assessment: petechiae and purpura ↗
    • Describe the distribution and morphology of bruising or purpura and assess whether lesions are palpable Technique card pending
  3. 03

    Oral blood blisters or gingival bleeding, conjunctival pallor, and jaundice

    • Oral and pharyngeal inspection and soft palate movement ↗
    • Inspection of the lower palpebral conjunctiva and eyes for pallor or jaundice ↗
  4. 04

    Palpate lymph nodes and assess for hepatosplenomegaly

    • Cervical lymph node palpation ↗
    • Liver palpation with respiration ↗
    • Splenic palpation in the right lateral position ↗
    • Axillary lymph nodes: arm support and palpation by group ↗
  5. 05

    Signs of liver disease, including spider angiomas, palmar erythema, and ascites

    Select as clinically indicated

    • Shifting dullness ↗
    • Inspect for spider angiomas and palmar erythema Technique card pending
  6. 06

    Joint swelling and warmth suggesting hemarthrosis

    Select as clinically indicated

    • Technique card pending
