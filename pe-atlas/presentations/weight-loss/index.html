Prioritize the patient's condition. Unexplained weight loss with dysphagia, bleeding, persistent fever or night sweats, or a lump requires further assessment. With shortness of breath, altered consciousness, or circulatory instability, prioritize urgent assessment.

Physical examination checklists by clinical presentation

Examinations without a card remain in the checklist. An item that combines several examinations may include both available cards and specific techniques that are still pending. What you actually examine depends on the symptoms and clinical indications.

  1. 01

    Measure height and weight, compare with previous records, and assess vital signs

    • Upper-arm blood pressure: standard measurement and comparison of both arms ↗
    • Adult anthropometric assessment, including waist and neck circumference Technique card pending
    • Temperature measurement Technique card pending
  2. 02

    Assess conjunctival pallor, scleral icterus, and the mouth, including teeth and ulcers

    • Oral and pharyngeal inspection and soft palate movement ↗
    • Inspection of the lower palpebral conjunctiva and eyes for pallor or jaundice ↗
  3. 03

    Palpate the thyroid and assess for hand tremor and proptosis

    • Thyroid palpation from behind while the patient swallows ↗
    • Thyroid-associated eye disease and eyelid signs Technique card pending
    • Observe for tremor Technique card pending
  4. 04

    Palpate cervical, supraclavicular, and axillary lymph nodes

    • Cervical lymph node palpation ↗
    • Axillary lymph nodes: arm support and palpation by group ↗
  5. 05

    Auscultate the lungs and palpate the abdomen for masses or hepatomegaly

    • Posterior lung auscultation sequence ↗
    • Light and deep abdominal palpation ↗
    • Liver palpation with respiration ↗
  6. 06

    Perform a digital rectal examination if indicated

    Select as clinically indicated

    • Digital rectal examination: consent, preparation, and left lateral positioning ↗
    • Digital rectal examination: rectal wall and prostate ↗
    • Assessment of potential bleeding causes as indicated by the circumstances Technique card pending
