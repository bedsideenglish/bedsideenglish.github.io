Prioritize the patient's condition. Rapidly altered consciousness, fever, suspected intoxication, or a specific risk of harm to self or others requires prompt safety and physical assessment. Do not infer violence risk from hallucinations or delusions alone.

Physical examination checklists by clinical presentation

Examinations without a card remain in the checklist. An item that combines several examinations may include both available cards and specific techniques that are still pending. What you actually examine depends on the symptoms and clinical indications.

  1. 01

    Appearance and hygiene, behavior such as agitation or responding to voices, and eye contact

    • General inspection from the end of the bed ↗
  2. 02

    Rate and amount of speech, including pressured speech; mood and affect

    • General inspection from the end of the bed ↗
    • Systematic interview assessment of speech and affect Technique card pending
  3. 03

    Thought process, including loose associations or flight of ideas, and thought content, including delusions or thoughts of harm to self or others

    • Technique card pending
  4. 04

    Assess whether hallucinations are currently present

    • Technique card pending
  5. 05

    Orientation, attention, judgment, and insight

    • Technique card pending
  6. 06

    Assess vital signs and perform a brief neurologic examination for physical causes

    Select as clinically indicated

    • Radial pulse: rate and rhythm ↗
    • Pupillary light reflex and swinging flashlight test (RAPD) ↗
    • Pronator drift test ↗
    • Upper-arm blood pressure: standard measurement and comparison of both arms ↗
    • Temperature measurement Technique card pending
