Prioritize the patient's condition. If recent self-harm, ingestion of medication, altered consciousness, or immediate risk of self-harm is suspected, prioritize physical and psychosocial safety assessment and access to help. Assess the individual circumstances; do not decide on admission or discharge solely from physical examination findings or a risk score.
