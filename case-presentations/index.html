Quick answer

What is an oral case presentation?

An oral case presentation is a concise, audience-specific summary of a patient’s story, relevant findings, clinical assessment, and proposed next steps. It should show how the facts support your reasoning—not reproduce every line of the chart. Its exact length and order should follow the setting and your supervisor’s expectations.

How to use the library

Read less. Retrieve more.

  1. 1

    Read the presentation brief and hide the model script.

  2. 2

    Give a 60–120 second presentation aloud in your own words.

  3. 3

    Compare sequence, clinical compression, uncertainty, and language.

  4. 4

    Answer the attending questions without rereading the script.

Fictional and deliberately incomplete.

Each guide is source-locked to a simulated patient case. Missing vital signs, examination findings, allergies, or tests stay missing, so you can practice stating uncertainty instead of inventing a polished chart.
