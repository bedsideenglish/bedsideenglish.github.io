Quick answer

What is healthcare team communication?

Healthcare team communication is the clear exchange of patient information, concern, responsibility, and next steps among clinicians. Structured tools such as SBAR help a sender organize a message; a check-back helps the receiver and sender confirm that the plan was understood.
