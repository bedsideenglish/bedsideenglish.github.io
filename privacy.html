Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated 2026-07-28 · Applies to both the Windows Desktop app and the Android app ("Bedside English: Talk & Train", internal codename "MedVoiceTrainer") · Developer: Kyeongeon Lee (Google Play developer name: KYEONGEON LEE)

Short version: Bedside English does not run any server and has no user accounts. Your practice history, transcripts, and settings stay in a local database on your own device. When you use live AI voice, AI feedback, or pronunciation scoring, your speech/text is sent directly from your device to the AI provider you personally configured (e.g. Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, or Microsoft Azure Speech) — never through a Bedside English server, because there isn't one.

We don't show ads, don't include advertising or analytics SDKs, and don't sell or use your data for advertising.

1. Who this policy covers

Bedside English: Talk & Train is a personal, open-source project built by an individual developer (contact below), distributed free of charge on GitHub and, for Android, on Google Play. It is an educational speaking-practice tool for medical and everyday English — not a clinical system, medical device, diagnostic service, or substitute for professional medical advice — and it is not affiliated with OET, OSCE, USMLE, or any residency-matching organization.

2. Data that stays on your device

The app stores the following locally (SQLite/Room database on Android; a local data file on Desktop) and never uploads it to any Bedside English server, because none exists:

  • Session history: transcripts, AI-generated responses, scores, fluency measurements, feedback, corrections, and checklist coverage.
  • Spaced-repetition (SRS) review items and mistake-review history, plus listening-drill answers and ratings.
  • Debrief content, including SOAP-style notes and commitments generated from your sessions.
  • Your preferences: interface and native language, selected AI/voice/speech provider and model, playback and notification settings, onboarding status.
  • Your API keys/credentials for Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, and/or Azure Speech, if you choose to enter them. On Android these are stored using encrypted preferences backed by the Android Keystore. On Desktop they are stored in a local configuration file in the app's own data folder on your device.
  • Optional recorded audio clips of your own practice speech, saved only if a "save voice clips" feature is available and enabled — viewable and deletable by you at any time, and never uploaded by the app itself. Temporary audio files may also be created in the app cache while analysis is being performed.
  • API token-usage and estimated cost records, calculated locally from your own provider usage.

On Android, the local database, encrypted credential preferences, and the learner-audio folder are explicitly excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data from your device's app settings, permanently deletes all of the above. There is no separate remote copy for us to delete, because we never received one.

3. Microphone and audio access

The app requests microphone access only when a feature needs spoken input — live voice conversation, dictation, pronunciation analysis, or another voice-based exercise. Depending on the feature and your settings, microphone audio may be streamed live to the AI/speech provider you configured, or a short clip may be sent for transcription or pronunciation scoring. You can deny or revoke microphone permission through your OS settings; the app will fall back to text-based practice where available, but voice features will not work without it. The app never activates the microphone for advertising or background monitoring unrelated to the exercise you started.

4. Data sent to the AI provider you configure

Live voice practice, transcription, AI feedback, and pronunciation scoring require an AI or speech model. The app does not include or resell access to any AI provider — you supply your own API key/credential for the provider(s) you want to use. When you start a session or request feedback, your speech audio, transcript, and/or relevant exercise context is sent directly from your device to that provider's own servers, under that provider's own privacy policy and terms, which we do not control:

If you don't configure a given provider, no data is ever sent to it. Demo/mock modes run entirely offline and contact no AI provider at all. Providers may process your data in countries other than your own, and their retention, logging, and model-improvement practices are governed by their own policies and your own account with them — review the provider's policy before enabling it or submitting sensitive information.

We recommend practicing with fictional or de-identified scenarios and never entering real patients' or third parties' identifiable information into a session (see §9).

4.1 YouTube transcript lookup

If you paste a public YouTube video URL or ID to import a caption track (for example, for a "teach-back" exercise), the app connects to YouTube to retrieve that video's public page and available captions. YouTube/Google may see normal network request information associated with that request, such as your device's IP address. See policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Anonymous usage telemetry (opt-in, currently inactive)

The app includes an optional telemetry mechanism for coarse usage statistics. It sends nothing unless you explicitly opt in and the developer has configured a telemetry endpoint and key in that build. As of this policy's effective date, no telemetry endpoint is configured in the shipped release of either app, so telemetry currently sends nothing regardless of your consent setting.

If it is ever activated in a future release, telemetry is designed to include only: a random per-install identifier (no account, no device identifier); the event name; app version and OS family; interface/native-language codes; a coarse country code derived from your system locale (never GPS or IP-based geolocation); session mode, duration, counts, and rounded score information; and limited technical error information (exception type and an in-app file/line location, never the error message text). It is designed to never include microphone audio, transcripts, prompts, feedback text, names, API keys, or any other free-form content you typed or spoke. Any future activation will be reflected in an updated "Last updated" date on this page and, for the Android app, in the Google Play Data safety section.

6. No accounts, no ads, no data sale

There is no sign-up, login, or Bedside English cloud profile on either platform. The apps do not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone — we simply never receive it in the first place. The Android app does not request access to contacts, precise location, call logs, SMS messages, photos, or the advertising identifier.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Sessions and learning records: stay on your device until you delete them, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Deleting a session in the app moves it to an in-app Trash area first; from there you can delete it immediately, or use the app's trash-cleanup control to purge items older than 7 days, which also deletes any audio clips saved for that session.
  • API credentials and settings: remain on your device until you remove or replace them, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.
  • Temporary files: cache/export audio files are kept only as long as the relevant feature needs them, subject to your OS's normal storage and cleanup behavior.
  • Third-party provider data: anything sent to Google, OpenAI, Anthropic, Microsoft, or YouTube is retained and deleted according to that provider's own policies and your account/configuration with them. We generally cannot access or delete data already sent to your own third-party provider account on your behalf — use that provider's own privacy controls.

Because the app never creates a Bedside English account, there is no separate online account for us to delete. You can remove all local app data at any time by deleting items in-app, clearing stored API credentials, using your OS's "clear app data" option, or uninstalling the app.

8. Security

  • API credentials are stored encrypted (Android Keystore-backed encrypted preferences on Android) or in a local, per-device configuration file (Desktop).
  • Learning records live in the app's private local database; learner audio clips live in the app's private local file storage.
  • Requests to AI/speech providers use HTTPS or secure WebSocket connections where the provider supports them.
  • On Android, the database, encrypted credential store, and learner-audio folder are excluded from automatic cloud backup.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Please also protect your own device and provider account credentials.

9. Sensitive medical or personal information

Bedside English is intended for simulated education and language practice. Please do not enter, speak, paste, or otherwise submit real patient-identifying information, protected health information, confidential clinical records, or other information you're not authorized to disclose. The app is not a HIPAA-compliant or equivalent clinical information system, and anything you voluntarily include in a voice session, transcript, or pasted material may be transmitted to the third-party AI/speech provider you've selected (§4).

10. Children and intended audience

Bedside English and its AI integrations are designed for adult learners — medical students, graduates, and residency applicants generally aged 18 or older — and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a Bedside English account or developer-operated backend, because none exists. A parent or guardian who believes a minor has used the app to provide information to a third-party AI provider should contact that provider directly and may also contact us at the address below.

11. Your choices

  • Use offline or demo/mock features without an API key or microphone access, where available.
  • Deny or revoke microphone and notification permissions in your OS settings.
  • Choose which third-party AI/speech provider(s) to use, or use none at all.
  • Remove or replace stored provider credentials at any time.
  • Opt in or out of anonymous telemetry (currently inactive regardless of this setting — see §5).
  • Delete individual sessions/learning records, or clear all local app data / uninstall the app.

Disabling a permission or removing a provider credential may prevent the related feature from working.
