Bedside English

About

Speak with more confidence.

Bedside English is a free, open-source tool for practising spoken English in clinical conversations.

What you can use

An AI-patient practice app and short guides for patient questions, model histories, case presentations, team communication, and everyday English.

Who it is for

Medical students, international medical graduates, and healthcare professionals who want words they can use aloud.

Educational use only

Bedside English supports communication practice. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations.
